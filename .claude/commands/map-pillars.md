# Map Pillars

You are running the **Pillar Mapper** for the Domain Intelligence Framework.

## Context

Read the following before proceeding:
- All session syntheses in `/sessions/`
- The problem map at `/outputs/problem-map.md`

The goal is to map the underlying structure of the SME's domain — not just what they know, but how that knowledge is organized and where it creates genuine, hard-to-replicate advantages.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Identify the Pillars
A knowledge pillar is a distinct cluster of expertise that:
- Has its own vocabulary and mental models
- Takes meaningful time to develop (months or years)
- Creates real asymmetry between those who have it and those who don't

Look for pillars in how the SME explained their world, taught you concepts, and described what separates good practitioners from great ones.

Typical domains have 4–8 pillars.

### Step 2: Document each pillar

```markdown
## Pillar: [Name]

**What it is:**
[Plain-language definition. How would you explain this to a smart non-expert?]

**Why it matters:**
[What goes wrong when someone doesn't understand this? What becomes possible when they do?]

**Common misconceptions:**
[What does the market misunderstand about this pillar? Where do outsiders get it wrong?]

**SME's edge:**
[What does this particular SME know about this pillar that most practitioners don't? What's the non-obvious insight?]

**Opportunity signal:**
[How does the gap between what the market understands and what the expert knows create a problem to solve?]

**Connected problems:**
[List the problem IDs from the problem map that live in this pillar]
```

### Step 3: Map pillar relationships
Some pillars depend on others. Some are foundational. Document the structure:
- Which pillars are prerequisites for others?
- Which pillars, if removed, would make the others meaningless?
- Where do pillars overlap in ways that create unique compound expertise?

### Step 4: Flag opportunity intersections
The highest-signal opportunities live where:
1. A high-priority problem (from the problem map) sits inside a pillar where the SME has a genuine edge
2. The market misconceptions around that pillar make existing solutions inadequate

Mark these intersections explicitly — they are the focus of the GTM canvas.

## Output

Write to `/outputs/knowledge-pillars.md`.

Append a section at the bottom: **"High-Signal Intersections"** — the top 2–3 places where a problem × pillar × edge creates a compelling opportunity.
