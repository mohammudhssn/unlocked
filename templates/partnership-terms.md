# Partnership Terms Template

> A simple, plain-language agreement for co-build partnerships. Not legal advice — get a lawyer for anything material.

This template is designed to be readable by a non-lawyer SME. Simple language, clear terms, no surprises. Use it to document the partnership before you build anything together.

---

```markdown
# Partnership Agreement — Unlocked Co-Build

**Date:** [DATE]
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Parties

**Builder:** Mohammud Hssn
Contact: [email]

**Domain Expert:** [Full Name]
Title: [Current role, institution]
Contact: [email]

---

## What We're Building

After completing a Domain Intelligence Framework engagement in the domain of **[DOMAIN]**, the parties have identified the following opportunity to co-build:

**Output type:** [Micro-tool / Course / SOP Pack / Productized Service / Startup]

**Description:** [2–3 sentences describing what the product is and who it serves]

**Primary audience:** [ICP — who buys or uses this]

---

## What Each Party Contributes

### Builder (Mohammud)
- Conducts the full DIF engagement (sessions, research, synthesis)
- Produces all structured outputs (problem map, knowledge pillars, GTM canvas)
- Designs and builds the product
- Manages distribution strategy and platform operations
- Handles ongoing product maintenance

### Domain Expert ([NAME])
- Participates in [N] structured sessions
- Reviews and validates outputs for domain accuracy
- Shares the product with their professional network upon launch
- Lends their name and credibility to the product (with approval rights on how they're represented)

---

## Revenue Share

All net revenue generated from **[PRODUCT NAME]** will be split:

| Party | Share |
|-------|-------|
| Builder (Mohammud) | 50% |
| Domain Expert ([NAME]) | 50% |

**Net revenue** is defined as gross revenue minus direct operating costs:
- Hosting and infrastructure
- Payment processing fees (typically 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction)
- Any agreed external contractors (approved by both parties in writing)

Revenue is paid out [monthly / quarterly] via [payment method — e.g. Stripe, bank transfer].

---

## Equity (Startup Track Only)

*Complete this section only if the output is a funded startup. Leave blank otherwise.*

If the partnership produces a company (incorporated as [ENTITY NAME / TBD]):

| Party | Initial equity | Vesting |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| Mohammud Hssn | [X]% | 4 years, 1-year cliff |
| [Domain Expert] | [Y]% | 4 years, 1-year cliff |

Additional equity pools (advisors, future hires) to be agreed before any fundraising round.

---

## Intellectual Property

All outputs produced during the DIF engagement — including session notes, problem maps, knowledge pillar documents, GTM canvases, SOP packs, and any derivative products — are **jointly owned** by both parties.

Neither party may sell, license, or transfer the joint IP without written consent from the other.

The Domain Expert retains ownership of their pre-existing knowledge and professional experience. The structured outputs produced via the DIF methodology are jointly owned as described above.

---

## Product Decisions

Major product decisions (pricing changes >20%, platform changes, new feature additions, partnership agreements with third parties) require agreement from both parties.

Day-to-day product and operational decisions are made by the Builder.

---

## The Guarantee

If after completing [4 / N] structured sessions no validated product opportunity is identified that both parties agree is worth pursuing:

- The Domain Expert is not obligated to continue
- The Domain Expert keeps all outputs produced (problem map, knowledge pillars, any session syntheses)
- The Builder does not charge for the engagement

If a product opportunity IS identified and the Domain Expert chooses not to pursue it, the Builder retains the right to explore it independently. The Domain Expert's personal name and specific identity will not be used without their explicit consent.

---

## Term and Termination

This agreement begins on **[DATE]** and continues until the product is discontinued by mutual agreement, or until one party provides 30 days written notice of intent to exit.

On termination:
- Revenue share continues for 12 months on revenue generated by the product before termination
- After 12 months, the continuing party assumes full ownership and revenue
- Any equity positions in a startup track are governed by the company's shareholder agreement, not this document

---

## Amendments

Any changes to this agreement must be made in writing and signed (digitally or physically) by both parties.
```
