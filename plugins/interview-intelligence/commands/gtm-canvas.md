# GTM Canvas

You are running the **GTM Canvas Generator** for the Domain Intelligence Framework.

## Context

Read the full knowledge vault before proceeding:
- All session syntheses in `/sessions/`
- Problem map at `/outputs/problem-map.md`
- Knowledge pillars at `/outputs/knowledge-pillars.md`
- Insight brief at `/outputs/insight-brief.md` (if it exists)
- Solution space notes from Sessions 4–6

Pay particular attention to:
- The "who would you call" moments in session notes — these surface the real ICP
- The SME's instinctive reactions to hypothetical solutions — their gut is data
- The high-signal intersections identified in the pillar mapper output

## Instructions

Generate a GTM canvas for the top-priority opportunity identified in the knowledge vault.

### 1. Opportunity Statement
One clear sentence: what is the problem, who has it, and why now is the right time to address it?

### 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
Don't build a persona from demographics. Build it from the SME's social graph and described experience.

- **Who they are:** Role, context, environment
- **What they're responsible for:** The outcome they're measured on
- **How this problem shows up in their day:** Specific, concrete moment of pain
- **What they've already tried:** Why existing solutions didn't stick
- **Where to find them:** Communities, events, publications, platforms
- **What they trust:** Who or what influences their decisions

### 3. Positioning
Answer the two questions a prospect asks the moment they encounter this:
- "Is this for me?" — The specificity signal
- "Why would I trust this?" — The credibility signal

Positioning statement format:
> For [ICP] who [problem they face], [product/service name] is the [category] that [key benefit] — unlike [alternative] which [limitation].

Generate 2–3 positioning angles and flag the strongest one based on the SME's edge.

### 4. Channel Hypotheses
Where does the ICP already go to learn, connect, and make decisions? Rank by:
- **Reach:** How many of the right people are there?
- **Trust:** How much do they trust what they encounter there?
- **Friction:** How easy is it to show up authentically?

Suggest 3 channels with a specific first action for each.

### 5. Pricing Hypotheses
Based on what the SME said about value, compensation, and what "good enough" looks like in their field, generate 3 pricing hypotheses:
- **Entry:** What would feel like a no-brainer?
- **Standard:** What feels fair for the full value delivered?
- **Premium:** What would people pay if the outcome was guaranteed?

### 6. First Move
The single highest-leverage action to take in the next 14 days to validate this opportunity. It should:
- Involve talking to a real potential customer (not more research)
- Produce a binary signal: yes this is real, or no it isn't
- Cost nothing but time

### 7. Risk Assumptions (Top 3)
The three beliefs this GTM canvas depends on that, if wrong, would invalidate the whole thing. These are the first things to test.

## Output

Write to `/outputs/gtm-canvas.md`.

The SME should be able to read this and immediately know their next 3 actions.
