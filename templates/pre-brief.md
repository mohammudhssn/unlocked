# Domain Pre-Brief Template

> Share this with the SME before Session 1. It signals credibility and primes deeper conversation.

**Purpose:** The Pre-Brief tells the expert: you've done the surface-level work. Now let's talk about what's underneath it. The first session starts deeper, faster.

**Time to produce:** 30–60 minutes using the research stack. See [sops/research-sop.md](../sops/research-sop.md).

## Research Stack Checklist

Before finalizing the Pre-Brief, run through:

**Layer 1 — Surface (30 min, Perplexity AI):**
- [ ] Market size + growth rate
- [ ] Top 5 incumbents + what they're failing at
- [ ] Recent funding activity (Crunchbase)
- [ ] Regulatory context
- [ ] Top complaints on Reddit / forums / professional communities
- [ ] Practitioner needs vs. what products exist

**Layer 2 — Deep Signal (MCP-powered, 30 min):**
- [ ] GitHub: open-source tools in this domain (what's being hacked together?)
- [ ] ArXiv/PubMed: what researchers have identified as gaps
- [ ] LinkedIn: who are the 50 loudest domain experts? What do they write about?
- [ ] Crunchbase/SEC: what's been tried, what failed

**Formatting:**
- [ ] 2 pages max
- [ ] Written in plain language — no jargon the expert doesn't use
- [ ] Ends with the "provocation" — one hypothesis that invites reaction
- [ ] Sent 24–48 hours before Session 1

**After the engagement:**
Store the Pre-Brief in the central brain at `/domains/[domain]/pre-brief.md`. Every future engagement in an adjacent domain starts with this as context.
