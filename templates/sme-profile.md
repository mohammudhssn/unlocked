# SME Network Profile Template

> One file per expert in `/sme-network/[expert-name].md`. This is the long-term relationship record.

The SME network is one of the most valuable assets in the Unlocked model. Every expert you engage is a distribution channel, a warm intro path, and a potential future co-build partner. This file tracks the relationship over time — not just the engagement.
