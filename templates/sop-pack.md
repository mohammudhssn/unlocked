# SOP Pack Template

> For codifying expert knowledge into a transferable, sellable process document.

An SOP Pack is one of the most underrated outputs of a DIF engagement. It's produced after Session 3 (Knowledge Mapping), using the pillar taxonomy as its skeleton. It turns tacit expertise into a documented, transferable process — and that process is a product.

**Run `/generate-sop` in Claude Code after Session 3 to produce the first draft automatically.**

---

## When to Produce an SOP Pack

- The expert's process is highly repeatable but rarely documented
- Junior practitioners in their field consistently make the same mistakes
- The expert informally teaches this process to peers (proof of demand)
- The engagement's Output Track points toward Course or Productized Service
- The SOP can be a standalone product AND the foundation for a larger build

## How the SOP Pack Becomes a Product

**As a standalone document:**
- PDF or Notion page
- Sold to domain peers at $97–297
- The expert shares it with their network → first buyers are their colleagues

**As the foundation for a course:**
- Each step becomes a module
- The "Expert Insight" sections become the anchor content
- The Quick Reference becomes the course cheatsheet

**As a startup's internal playbook:**
- The SOP is the first version of the company's internal documentation
- New hires learn from it, not from the founder's head
- It's the IP that survives the expert stepping back

**As a consulting asset:**
- The productized service version: you deliver the output of Step 1–5 as a fixed-scope service
- The SOP is what makes it repeatable and scalable beyond the original expert

## Using `/generate-sop` in Claude Code

After Session 3, run:

```
/generate-sop
```

Claude will:
1. Read your session notes and pillar taxonomy from the vault
2. Identify the process with the highest tacit knowledge density
3. Generate a first-draft SOP using this template
4. Flag sections where more expert input is needed

Review and fill in the gaps in Session 4 or async with the expert.
