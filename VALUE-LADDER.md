## Tier Breakdown

### FREE: Lead Magnets (Email Capture Only)

Three tools that demonstrate the DIF methodology without requiring an engagement. Build each with Claude API + a simple form UI. Host at `tools.mohammud.com` or `mohammud.com/unlocked/tools`.

**Tool 1: Domain Question Generator**
- Input: a domain name (e.g. "construction project management")
- Output: a structured 20-question interview set organized by knowledge type (Explicit / Tacit / Latent)
- Why it works: shows the depth of the methodology. Consultants, researchers, and accelerators use it daily — secondary audience, direct referral source.
- Build time: 1–2 days

**Tool 2: Problem Prioritizer**
- Input: a list of domain problems (free text)
- Output: each problem classified as Urgent / Important / Latent with reasoning + signal type tag
- Why it works: makes the DIF output tangible before an engagement. Someone runs their own problem list, sees the framework in action, wants the full session.
- Build time: 1–2 days

**Tool 3: GTM Fit Calculator**
- Input: 10 questions about a domain opportunity
- Output: fit score + recommended output track (micro-tool / course / SaaS / startup) + rationale
- Why it works: doubles as a lead qualifier. Only people with a real domain and real ambition spend 5 minutes answering 10 questions.
- Build time: 1–2 days

### HIGH-TICKET: Full Unlocked Engagement ($3,000–5,000)

The complete cycle. Sessions 1–6. Full output stack. Ends with a venture-ready package.

**What's included:**
- Everything in the Domain Intelligence Session
- Sessions 5–6: market validation + GTM co-creation
- Research enrichment layer (MCP-powered market research)
- GTM Canvas with ICP, positioning angles, channels, pricing hypotheses
- First distribution strategy + test designed
- Output track decision with rationale
- Knowledge vault built and MCP-ready (persistent asset)

**The guarantee:** If after 4 sessions we haven't identified at least one validated product opportunity, the engagement is free. The SME keeps the Problem Map and Knowledge Pillars either way.

**Why the guarantee works:** The process almost always reveals something. The risk isn't "we find nothing" — it's "we find something and don't build fast enough." That's a different problem, and a better one to have.

### PARTNERSHIP: Co-Build with Equity / Revenue Share

The highest-leverage engagement. Not consulting — co-building.

**The deal structure:**
- You run the full engagement (sessions, research, synthesis)
- Co-build the output together (micro-tool, course, or startup)
- Revenue split: 50/50 on output revenue
- Startup cases: co-founder structure with equity

**What the SME brings:** Context (their knowledge) · Credibility (their reputation validates the output) · Community (their network is the launch channel)

**What you bring:** Structure (DIF methodology) · Speed (AI compresses months to 2 weeks) · Build (you ship the product)

**Revenue math:** One micro-tool generating $3k/month across 10 partnerships = $30k/month recurring — without adding one more client conversation.

## The Hormozi Frame: Making Every Offer Irresistible

The value equation applies at every tier:

```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood of Success) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort)
```

**Dream Outcome:** Your expertise becomes a product that earns without trading time for money.

**Likelihood of Success:** The TAC case study + FFA research proves this works across domains. The guarantee removes residual risk.

**Time Delay → Attack this hard:**
- DIF compresses expertise extraction from years to 2 weeks
- The Discovery Bot runs the surface-level questions before the first live session — you walk in already 30 minutes deep

**Effort / Sacrifice → Attack this harder:**
- The expert just talks. You structure, synthesize, and build.
- The AI stack handles what would normally take a team of researchers and writers
- The SME's only job: show up to four conversations

**The value stack (partnership tier):**

| Deliverable | Standalone Value |
|---|---|
| Domain Intelligence Report | $5,000+ |
| Problem Map with priority signals | $2,000 |
| Knowledge Pillar Taxonomy | $1,500 |
| GTM Canvas | $2,000 |
| Micro-tool build or course outline | $3,000+ |
| Revenue share on output | Unlimited upside |
| **Total value** | **$13,500+** |
| **Partnership price** | **$0 upfront — rev share only** |

The partnership tier is the offer that closes. The free tools generate the leads. The mid-ticket session proves the methodology. The partnership is where value compounds.

## The Content Engine: Epiphany Bridges

The way you sell each tier is not by pitching the product — it's by sharing the revelation that makes the product obvious. This is the Epiphany Bridge (Russell Brunson).

**For the free tools:** "I discovered you could map a domain expert's knowledge in 20 questions. Here's the template."

**For the intelligence session:** "I ran 4 conversations with an anesthesiologist and found a $2M problem hiding in plain sight. Here's what the process looked like."

**For the partnership:** "I've built a system that turns your expertise into a product. You bring the knowledge. I build the thing. We split what it earns."

The content that drives this is:
- **LinkedIn long-form:** Case studies framed as what I learned, not what I sell
- **The Unlocked essay** on mohammud.com — the manifesto that makes the worldview compelling before the product is introduced
- **Short video** (the Remotion explainer at mohammud.com/unlocked) — the hook that drives to the essay and the tools

Every SME who goes through the process shares a version of their output with their domain peers. Their 2,000 LinkedIn followers are all potential next SMEs. This is built-in distribution — no paid acquisition required at the early stage.
